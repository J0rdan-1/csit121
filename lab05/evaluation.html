UI/UX Site Evaluation: University Of Baltimore

The website I will be evaluating is the school I am transfering to next year, which is the University of Baltimore
The target audience of this website is people interested in the University of Baltimore, more specifically, their simulation and game design major.
I would say the way this site is organized, it's more linear than random.
One CRAP element I have observed is Proximity because looking at the site, all relevant information pertaing to a specifc topic is grouped together.
ACcording tho the accesibilty tracker the website was given a 78% with 2 elements that may cause issues with specific disabilities.
The site definitely helps users complete tasks accurately and quickly with tabs and menus to make the website user friendly.
I think one recommendation I would make is slightly changing the layout to make some content more vsibile to the user.
