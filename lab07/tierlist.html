Tier Grade The Movie Story Realism Car Selection Ending
S Fast and Furious: Tokyo Drift Amazing Probably among the best Genuinely the best car selection of any movie in the series. Peak Cinema
A 2 Fast 2 Furious Also a really good entry to the series A couple scenes were questionable but fully grounded still I loved the car selection in this movie Ending was great
B The Fast and The Furious The First in the series and one of the best stories to date The realest in the serious with only a few minor issues ICONIC car selection taht catipulted cars like the Toyota Supra Also the most iconic ending in the series
