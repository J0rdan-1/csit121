How Is It Used?

PMIs are used to specify which payment rail, app, or instrument is supported or used during transactions.
As stated before this is very important for online shopping spaces.
Merchants create a list of accepted PMIs and hand it off to the API so that the user and browser knows what payment methods are accepted.
That is one way PMIs are used.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Matching user handlers is also a very important use of PMIs.
In this case the user agent checks which payment apps are installed or credentials and matches them to the requested identifiers.
The system then presents eligible options to the user. One more use of PMIs wouldbe decentralized routing and trust or URL-based PMIs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

URL-based PMIs let payment providers publish payment manifest and security/handler info on the domains they control.
Digital cards and networks are one example of PMIs in use. Things like Apple Pay, Google Pay, Chase Pay, etc.
These are some examples of how PMIs are used.
PMIs are super important to modenr life and without them life on the internet wouldn't be as simple and easy
