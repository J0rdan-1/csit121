Why Is It Important?

PMIs are importnat becuase they are integral to online shopping.
Without these things, the world of online shopping would be virtually impossible becuase PMIs lay the groundwork for these systems.
It benifits not only the consumer but also the sellers.
Without PMIs it would be a lot harder to sell things online.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These systems allow for really easy transactions between multiple different systems without having to vhange the API.
All they have to do is declare which payment methods they support and the system handles everything else.
It also allows for more payment methods to be intorduced down the line with little to know resistance.
They are also important because consistent formats are importnat in order to prevent ambiguity when matching what a selkler accpets verus what a person's wallet provides.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Its alos importnat becuase it makes the whole process more transparent and easier to follow on both ends.
In order to simplify this somewhat chaotic mess you give each transaction its own unique identifer.
By giving each transaction its own identifer you reduce confusion basically tenfold because each transaction is uniquely identifiable by its given id.
That is why PMIs are important.
